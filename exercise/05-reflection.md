## Reflection

I trust AI to systematically review an ADR, identify inconsistencies, and explain technical risks in a structured way. For example, during the review, the AI highlighted that email demand at 200 orders per second exceeds MailFast's 50-email-per-second limit, causing the backlog to grow during sustained peak traffic. However, AI findings still need to be verified against the actual scenario and ADR. The 30-minute peak example discussed by the group was an assumption, not a stated requirement; the group-reviewed report labels it as inferred. I remain responsible for accurately presenting the review to the board, distinguishing evidence from assumptions, and avoiding unsupported conclusions. AI is useful for systematic analysis, but humans must verify its claims and make the final decisions.
